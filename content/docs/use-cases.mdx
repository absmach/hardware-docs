# Use Cases

The A0 IoT Gateway is an ESP32-C6 (RISC-V, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5 LE, 802.15.4) base board with two expansion slots (BUS1/BUS2) for SIM7080G (NB-IoT/LTE-M + GNSS), WMBUS (Wireless M-Bus metering), and E07-400M10S (sub-GHz 400 MHz) modules. Power via USB-C, external supply, or battery with charging. Software: Zephyr RTOS, WAMR WebAssembly edge logic, Magistrala (MQTT/CoAP/HTTP/WebSockets) integration, with secure boot, flash encryption, and TEE.

## Smart Metering

### Residential Utility Monitoring

**Challenge**: Remotely monitor heat and water usage across apartment blocks with Wireless M-Bus meters.

**Solution**:

- Deploy A0 gateway per block
- Read meters via WMBUS module — no rewiring
- Backhaul via NB-IoT (SIM7080G)
- Publish to Magistrala over MQTT
- Battery-backed operation for resilience

**Benefits**:

- No truck rolls for meter reading
- Real-time consumption monitoring
- Leak detection and anomaly alerts

### District Heating Network

**Challenge**: Monitor temperature, flow, and energy across distributed heat meters.

**Solution**:

- A0 gateways at strategic points
- Collect from Wireless M-Bus heat meters
- Edge filtering/aggregation via Wasm workloads
- Cellular backhaul where no wired connectivity exists

**Benefits**:

- Optimized energy distribution
- Rapid leak detection
- Improved billing accuracy

## Industrial IoT

### Sensor Data Collection

**Challenge**: Collect sensor data in secure, bandwidth-constrained industrial environments.

**Solution**:

- A0 with Wi-Fi 6 / BLE / 802.15.4 for local sensor links
- Wasm edge logic for filtering and threshold alerts
- Encrypted uplink via NB-IoT or Wi-Fi to Magistrala
- OTA updates and remote management

**Benefits**:

- Secure edge-to-cloud communication
- Reduced uplink bandwidth via edge processing
- Local operation during network outages

### Facility Energy Monitoring

**Challenge**: Monitor energy consumption across a facility using wireless metering.

**Solution**:

- A0 gateways at distribution points
- Wireless M-Bus metering radios
- Real-time upload to building/energy management via Magistrala

**Benefits**:

- Identify energy waste
- Peak demand insight
- Cost optimization

## Smart Cities & Remote Monitoring

**Challenge**: Large-scale metering with NB-IoT and sub-GHz coverage.

**Solution**:

- WMBUS or sub-GHz (E07-400M10S) for meter collection
- NB-IoT backhaul to Magistrala
- GNSS (via SIM7080G) for asset geo-tagging where needed

## Deployment Configurations

### Standalone Gateway

- **Use**: Data collection over on-board Wi-Fi 6 / BLE / 802.15.4
- **Power**: USB-C, external supply, or battery with charging
- **Backhaul**: Add SIM7080G, WMBUS, or E07-400M10S module in BUS1/BUS2 as needed
- **Complexity**: Minimal

### Cellular Backhaul Gateway

- **Use**: Meter or sensor collection with NB-IoT/LTE-M uplink where no Wi-Fi exists
- **Modules**: WMBUS or E07-400M10S on BUS1 for collection, SIM7080G on BUS2 for backhaul
- **Power**: External supply with battery backup for resilience
- **Complexity**: Moderate — two modules share SPI/I2C, so stagger bus access in firmware

### Battery-Powered Field Node

- **Use**: Remote metering or asset tracking with GNSS geo-tagging
- **Modules**: SIM7080G alone (cellular + GNSS via J4 LTE and J3 GNSS antennas, nano-SIM in U3)
- **Power**: Battery on the JST connector with MCP73831 charging; budget for cellular transmit bursts (see [SIM7080G](./a0/modules/sim7080g#power-budget))
- **Complexity**: Low hardware, careful power budgeting required
